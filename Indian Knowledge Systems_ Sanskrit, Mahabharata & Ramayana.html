The LanguageSanskrit (संस्कृतम्)

Sanskrit means “perfected” or “refined”. It is one of the world’s oldest documented languages and the classical language of India, used for scripture, poetry, science, mathematics, medicine and philosophy. Many modern Indian languages draw heavily on its vocabulary, and it is closely tied to the study of Indo-European linguistics.

3500+years of recorded history
~4000sutras in Panini’s grammar
Devanagarimost common script today
2stages: Vedic & Classical

Vedic Sanskrit

The older form, found in the Rigveda and other Vedic hymns. It has distinct grammar, accents and meters.

Classical Sanskrit

Standardised by Panini’s Ashtadhyayi (around 4th century BCE). Used in the epics, drama and kavya poetry.

Scientific Precision

Its phonetic organisation and rule-based grammar inspire modern work in linguistics and computing.

वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम्

Itihasa · EpicThe Mahabharata (महाभारतम्)

Traditionally attributed to the sage Vyasa, the Mahabharata is among the longest epic poems in the world. It tells of the dynastic struggle between the Pandavas and Kauravas culminating in the Kurukshetra war, while exploring dharma, duty, power, loyalty and consequence.

~100,000shlokas (verses)
18parvas (books)
18days of war
700verses of the Bhagavad Gita

Key Characters

  • Yudhishthira, the dharma-bound eldest Pandava
  • Arjuna, the master archer
  • Krishna, guide and charioteer
  • Bhishma, Karna, Draupadi, Duryodhana

The Bhagavad Gita

Part of the Bhishma Parva. Krishna’s dialogue with Arjuna on the battlefield covers duty, action without attachment, devotion and knowledge, and remains one of the most widely read Indian texts.

Adi Kavya · EpicThe Ramayana (रामायणम्)

Composed by the sage Valmiki, the Ramayana is called the Adi Kavya, the first poem. It follows Prince Rama of Ayodhya: his exile, the abduction of Sita by Ravana, the alliance with Hanuman and the vanara army, and the return to Ayodhya.

~24,000shlokas
7kandas (sections)
Anushtubhprincipal meter

The story has been retold across Asia, in Tulsidas’s Ramcharitmanas, Kamban’s Tamil Ramavataram, and countless performances, dances and folk traditions.
